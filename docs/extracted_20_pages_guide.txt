Executive Summary & AI Voice Impersonation Threat

AI Voice Cloning Ka Khatra Kyun Badh Gaya Hai?

Aaj ke time par generative AI aur neural speech synthesis models (jaise ElevenLabs, XTTS v2, Bark aur VALL-E) itne advance ho chuke hain ki kisi bhi insaan ki aawaz ka sirf 3 second ka YouTube clip ya social media audio le kar uski exact twin voice clone ki ja sakti hai.

Attacking teams iss cloned voice ka use karke CEO Fraud, Bank Account Takeover, aur Emergency Impersonation calls karte hain. Bank call center ya employee ko lagta hai ki real CEO ya real account holder bol raha hai, aur wo bina

cross-check kiye lakhs rupees ka fund transfer authorize kar dete hain.

Purane Security Methods Kyun Fail Ho Rahe Hain?

Purane tareeqe jaise Caller ID Check (jo VoIP se seconds me spoof ho jata hai), manual call-back (jo emergency fakes me skip ho jata hai), aur human voice familiarity (insani kaan neural vocoders ke micro-artifacts nahi sun sakte)—ye sab generative AI attacks ke samne zero security dete hain.

Enterprise & Banking Par Impact

Agar real-time voice verification system nahi hoga, toh har mahine financial institutions aur public sector banks ko crore rupees ka loss hoga. Hame ek aisa security framework banana padega jo incoming call ko bina audio quality damage kiye live inspect kare.

Synthetic Voice Cloning Ki Scientific Theoretical Basis

Neural Text-to-Speech (TTS) Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?

Kisi bhi modern voice clone system me 2 core components hote hain:

Acoustic Model: Text script ko le kar pitch aur pronunciation ki mel-spectrogram image banata hai.

Neural Vocoder (HiFi-GAN / WaveGlow / BigVGAN): Mel-spectrogram ko time-domain sound wave me convert karta hai jise hum sunte hain.

Human Vocal Tract Vs Neural Vocoder Discrepancies

Insani vocal tract (gala, teeth, tongue, lungs) aawaz banate waqt physical airflow constraints follow karta hai. AI models in 4 physical areas me hamesha pakde jate hain:

Phase Inconsistency: Human glottal pulses mathematical phase alignment rakhte hain, jabki AI vocoders random phase noise generate karte hain.

Spectral Over-Smoothing: Deep learning models spectrogram ke barfiye formants ko smooth kar dete hain.

Unnatural Pitch Contours: AI voice ka F0 pitch track unnaturally smooth hota hai, isme insani breath micro-tremors miss hote hain.

Glottal Closure Residuals: AI lungs aur respiratory pause dynamics missing hote hain.

Multi-Layer Analysis: Layer 1 - Acoustic & Spectral Detection

Spectral Feature Extraction (CQCC & LFCC)

Layer 1 low-level audio signals ko scan karta hai. Standard MFCCs ke sath Constant Q Cepstral Coefficients (CQCC) aur Linear Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (LFCC) calculate kiye jate hain, jo high-frequency harmonics aur neural vocoder artifacts ko precise frequency resolution par pakadte hain.

Bispectrum & Bicoherence Phase Coupling Analysis

Bispectrum non-linear phase coupling ko measure karta hai. Real human vocal tract me non-linear interactions hote hain (bicoherence > 0.45). Neural vocoder se bani synthetic speech linear filter models par chalti hai, isliye iska bispectrum bicoherence phase decoupled hota hai.

Linear Prediction (LP) Residual Excitation

Audio stream ko inverse LPC filter se pass karke glottal residual excitation alag kiya jata hai. Real human audio me periodic sharp glottal spikes milte hain, jabki synthetic voice me high-frequency dispersion aur abnormal kurtosis milta hai.

Multi-Layer Analysis: Layer 2 - Prosody & Micro-Behavior

Fundamental Frequency (F0) Dynamics & Micro-Jitter

Layer 2 conversation ke pitch aur rhythm ko track karta hai. Real insani vocal cords me hamesha natural

micro-instabilities hoti hain jinhe jitter (frequency perturbation) aur shimmer (amplitude perturbation) kehte hain. AI speech models mathematically clean splines generate karte hain, jisse jitter value abnormally zero aati hai.

Pause Duration & Respiratory Breath Intakes

Real human dialogue me sentence bolne se pehle natural respiratory breath intakes (saans lene ki aawaz) aur 200ms–500ms ke cognitive pauses hote hain. Live AI bots ya prerecorded audio feeds me ya toh zero breathing hoti hai ya fixed artificial API delay pauses hote hain.

Stress-Pitch Disfluency Mismatch

Jab koi real caller ghabra kar ya urgency me bolta hai (e.g. urgent fund transfer), toh uski pitch mean, speed aur acoustic intensity teenon ek sath surge karti hain. Fake voice me urgency text script se bolne par pitch bina vocal tract tension ke surge karti hai—jo disfluency flag create karta hai.

Deep Learning SOTA Model Ensemble Pipeline

AASIST3 (Graph Neural Network Back-end)

Anti-spoofing with Adaptive Softmax and Instance-wise Temperature (AASIST3) audio spectrogram ko graph nodes me convert karta hai. Heterogeneous Graph Attention Networks (HS-GAT) temporal frames aur spectral sub-bands ke beech complex non-local relationships read karke synthetic vocoder boundaries detect karte hain.

RawNet3 (Raw Time-Domain Waveform Model)

RawNet3 audio ko spectrogram me convert karne ki jagah seedha raw 1D time-domain waveform par SincNet filterbanks chala kar operate karta hai. Isse phase information 100% preserve hoti hai jo standard STFT transforms me erase ho jati thi.

WavLM Self-Supervised Learning (SSL) Front-End

Pre-trained WavLM-Large model contextual phonetic speech features extract karta hai. Anti-spoofing projection head se fine-tune karke, WavLM noisy telephone lines par bhi synthetic anomalies instant pakad leta hai.

Real-Time Dynamic Risk Engine Math & Logic

Overlapping 160ms Sliding Window

Live audio ko 160ms duration frames me stride 40ms ke sath continuously chop karke analyze kiya jata hai. Engine last 10 seconds ka rolling memory state maintain karta hai taaki temporary line noise se false alarm na ho, lekin continuous AI voice aate hi 1 second ke andar score jump kar jaye.

Dynamic Kalman Filter Risk Smoothing

Raw neural prediction S_raw(t) par Kalman Filter apply karke smooth dynamic risk score R(t) nikala jata hai:

R(t) = R(t-1) + K(t) * [ S_raw(t) - R(t-1) ]

Bayesian Context Enrichment Multipliers

Base risk score me Call Metadata add kiya jata hai:

+0.20: Agar call anjaan VoIP range ya hidden SIP trunk se aa raha ho.

+0.25: Agar call me ■5,00,000+ ka wire transfer perform ho raha ho.

+0.15: Agar phone number past cybersecurity fraud database me blacklisted ho.

8kHz Telephony Codec Vs 16kHz HD Audio Robustness

PSTN Telephony Codec Degradation

Standard mobile phone calls G.711 (PCMA/PCMU) codec par 8kHz sample rate me kaam karte hain, jisse frequency range 4kHz par cap ho jati hai. Standard AI detectors jo 16kHz studio audio par trained hote hain, mobile phone calls par 18%+ Error Rate dene lagte hain.

Neural Bandwidth Extension (BWE) Super-Resolution

Humaari pipeline feature extraction se pehle Lightweight Neural Bandwidth Extension (BWE) layer run karti hai, jo 8kHz narrow-band signal me se lost 4kHz–8kHz high-frequency acoustic harmonics reconstruct kar leti hai.

Codec-Augmented Model Fine-Tuning

Models ko G.711, AMR-NB, AMR-WB aur Opus 8k codecs ke simulated distortion noise par train kiya gaya hai, jisse erratic mobile networks par bhi model robust chalta hai.
